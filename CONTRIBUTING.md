# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in improving Chord 2go Spotify.

## Scope

Please keep changes focused, reviewable, and directly relevant to the public
project. For the first public release, playback documentation and support must
remain accurate: **Spotify Connect uses Ogg Vorbis up to 320 kbps, not Spotify
Lossless**.

## Required for contributions

- Keep changes scoped to the problem being solved.
- Include or update tests when behavior changes.
- Prefer small pull requests with clear rationale.
- Update public documentation when user-visible behavior changes.

## Do not contribute

Do **not** submit or attach:

- proprietary binaries
- decompiled app source
- firmware images
- credentials, tokens, or secrets
- packet captures
- private IP addresses or personal network details
- copyrighted content you do not have rights to contribute
- material intended to bypass service restrictions or claim lossless Spotify
  support for this project

Do **not** add private reverse-engineering artifacts, unpublished updater
material, or anything that would expose device owners or third-party systems.

## Pull request guidelines

- Describe what changed and why.
- Note user-visible risks and rollback considerations.
- Keep commits and files limited to what the change needs.
- Be prepared to revise work that expands scope, weakens safety messaging, or
  introduces unsupported claims.

## Legal and safety

By contributing, you affirm that you have the right to submit the material and
that it does not include secrets, unauthorized proprietary content, or data from
devices you are not authorized to examine.
